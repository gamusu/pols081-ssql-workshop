POLS 081 · SSQL Workshop

SSQL office hours and other help for the POLS 081 data paper.

SSQL office hours

The Social Science Quantitative Lab (SSQL) helps with every stage of a data paper: finding data, cleaning and merging it, making figures and tables, running and interpreting tests, and getting R, Excel or Stata to cooperate.

  • Where: Science Center 205
  • Book a time with Greg: SSQL office hours

Bring: your question ladder (or your current question), a link to or copy of your data, and the file you’re working in. If something is broken, bring the exact error message.

Your professor’s office hours are another place to talk through your question and argument. The booking link is in the syllabus.

Software trouble

  1. Work through the troubleshooting section of the setup guide: Mac · Windows · Quarto and PDF.
  2. Still stuck? Email Greg with what you did, what you expected, and the exact error (a screenshot helps).

Help inside R and RStudio

  • ?mean opens the help page for a function (here, mean()). ??median searches all help pages.
  • The Help tab (bottom-right pane in RStudio) searches the documentation.
  • Free online books: R for Data Science (2e) and the Quarto guide.

A note on AI tools

The syllabus allows AI for feedback on work you’ve already done, but not for producing your graphs, tables or visualizations, and you must include an AI-use statement at the end of your paper. When you’re stuck on code, SSQL office hours are a good alternative.
