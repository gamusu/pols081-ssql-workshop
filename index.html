Working with Data in Global Environmental Governance

POLS 081 · Class Meeting #11 · Wednesday, November 18, 2026

A two-part session led by Greg Amusu of the Social Science Quantitative Lab (SSQL), built to support your data paper.

Schedule

Time Part
1:15–2:30 pm Part 1. Lecture and discussion. Epistemology, quantitative social science, and approaches to the data paper. We’ll work through three course readings in groups.
2:30–2:45 pm Break
2:45–4:00 pm Part 2. Lab. Hands-on data analysis in RStudio and Quarto: summary statistics, a figure, and a statistical test.

Before class: set up your computer

Everyone will use R, RStudio and Quarto in the lab, whatever software you plan to use for your paper. Please install them before Wednesday:

  1. Install R and RStudio: Mac guide · Windows guide
  2. Make your first Quarto document and render it to PDF: Quarto & PDF guide

If you get stuck, work through the troubleshooting section of each guide, then email Greg. Anything still unresolved, we’ll fix during the break.

Before class: the readings

Come ready to discuss all three papers in small groups. Pay particular attention to their methods (how they turn a concept into something measurable) and their results (what they can and can’t conclude).

  • Fransen et al. (2023), Taking stock of the implementation gap in climate policy
  • Xie et al. (2025), Tracing inclusivity at UNFCCC conferences through side events and interest group dynamics
  • Tennant, Davies & Tennant (2024), Determinants of access to climate finance: Nuanced insights for SIDS and other vulnerable economies
