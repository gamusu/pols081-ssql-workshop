Data Sources for Your Data Paper

POLS 081 · SSQL Workshop

Where to find data for the POLS 081 data paper: the syllabus list, sources by primary focus, and the data behind the course readings.
Modified

November 17, 2026

Your data paper needs a question you can answer with data you can actually get. This page collects places to look. Start with the section for your primary focus, then look at the data behind the course readings: if a paper you liked used a dataset, you may be able to use it too.

TipBefore you commit to a dataset, check four things
  1. Coverage: does it include the countries, organizations or years you need?
  2. Variation: does your measure actually differ across your cases or over time? (See the question ladder worksheet.)
  3. Definitions: read the codebook. What exactly is counted, and who produced the numbers?
  4. Access: can you download it as a spreadsheet (.csv or .xlsx) without paying or registering?

Sources listed in the syllabus

Source What you’ll find
UNFCCC Treaties, party submissions, NDCs, COP documents. Also the UNFCCC data interface for national greenhouse gas inventories
IMF Climate Change Indicators Dashboard Country indicators on emissions, climate finance, green bonds, environmental taxes and more
World Resources Institute data WRI’s datasets, including Climate Watch (emissions and NDC content by country)
Climate Action Tracker Ratings of countries’ targets and policies; projected emissions. Used by Fransen et al. (2023)
ND-GAIN Country Index Climate vulnerability and readiness scores for about 185 countries, every year since 1995
OECD Data Explorer OECD environment statistics and development finance, including the Rio markers for climate-related aid. The two OECD links in the syllabus point to pages that have moved; search the Data Explorer instead.
Germanwatch Climate Risk Index Rankings of how hard countries have been hit by extreme weather
World Bank Open Data: climate change Country indicators on emissions, energy, forests and more. The World Development Indicators DataBank lets you choose countries, indicators and years and download a spreadsheet
EPA: Selected multilateral environmental instruments in force for the U.S. List of treaties the United States has joined
InforMEA: treaties Multilateral environmental agreements, with parties and decisions
European Commission: environmental cooperation The EU’s environmental agreements and partnerships (the syllabus link now redirects here)

Data by primary focus

The syllabus asks you to choose one primary focus. Here are good starting points for each. Many sources work for more than one focus.

An actor

China, the United States, the EU, AOSIS, a multinational corporation, an NGO.

Source Useful for
Climate Watch Any country’s emissions over time and the content of its NDC
Our World in Data: CO2 and greenhouse gas emissions (download on GitHub) Emissions by country since 1750, per person, by fuel, consumption-based
EDGAR and the Global Carbon Budget The emissions estimates behind many other sources
UNFCCC party groupings Who belongs to AOSIS, the LDCs, the G77 and other negotiating groups
UNFCCC past conferences Lists of participants for each COP (delegation sizes, as in Kaya & Schofield 2020)
UNFCCC admitted observer organizations NGOs and other observers, by type and year of admission
CDP data Corporate climate disclosures (some data require registration)

Tip. A single actor gives you no variation across cases. Get variation from time (how has China’s position changed?), a comparison group (China vs. other large emitters), or parts of a total (what is the EU’s climate finance made of?).

An international organization or institution

The World Bank, Green Climate Fund, Global Environment Facility, AIIB, UNFCCC.

Source Useful for
World Bank projects and operations Every World Bank project, downloadable with sector, country, amount and dates
Green Climate Fund open data GCF projects: country, amount, mitigation vs. adaptation, public vs. private
GEF project database Global Environment Facility projects by focal area, country and agency
AIIB project list Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank projects
Climate Funds Update Pledges, deposits and approvals across multilateral climate funds
UNFCCC side events archive Side events at past COPs (the data behind Xie et al. 2025)

An issue

Climate finance, environmental foreign aid, adaptation, loss and damage, deforestation, renewable energy, critical minerals, fossil-fuel dependence.

Issue Sources
Climate finance and environmental aid OECD Data Explorer: search “Rio markers” or “climate-related development finance” (aid by donor, recipient, year and adaptation/mitigation) · Climate Funds Update · UNFCCC biennial reports
Adaptation and vulnerability ND-GAIN · Germanwatch Climate Risk Index
Loss and damage, disasters EM-DAT international disaster database (free for academic use after registering)
Deforestation Global Forest Watch (tree cover loss by country and year)
Renewable energy Ember electricity data · Our World in Data: energy · World Bank WDI (renewables share; note that it counts firewood and charcoal)
Critical minerals USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries (production and reserves by country)
Fossil-fuel dependence and subsidies World Bank WDI (fuel exports as % of merchandise exports; oil, gas and coal rents) · IMF fossil fuel subsidies
Environmental conflict Uppsala Conflict Data Program downloads

A governance instrument or policy

NDCs, carbon pricing, emissions trading, climate-related development finance, voluntary carbon markets, environmental provisions in trade agreements.

Instrument Sources
NDCs UNFCCC NDC registry (the documents) · Climate Watch (NDC content coded and comparable) · Climate Action Tracker (ratings)
Carbon pricing and emissions trading World Bank Carbon Pricing Dashboard · ICAP ETS map
Climate laws and policies Climate Change Laws of the World · Climate Policy Database · CCPI (policy scores; used by Fransen et al. 2023)
Climate-related development finance OECD Data Explorer (Rio markers)
Voluntary carbon markets Berkeley Carbon Trading Project: Voluntary Registry Offsets Database
Environmental provisions in trade agreements TREND: Trade and Environment Database

Tip. Policy questions often compare adopters and non-adopters, or before and after adoption. Remember from Part 1: that comparison is descriptive unless you have a design that rules out other explanations.

Data used in the course readings

Many course readings are built on data you can use too. The tables below cover readings that analyze quantitative data. Other readings cite statistics (for example, the Financial Times and WRI pieces) but don’t analyze a dataset.

Original data were collected or coded by the authors; secondary data come from an existing source. Entries marked † are based on the paper’s abstract or general knowledge of the source, so check the paper itself for details.

Original data you can’t download? Many authors share their data on request, and the methods section usually explains how they built it. A small version of their coding, done by you for a handful of cases, can make a good data paper.
